# Guild Legacy

Guild Legacy is a mobile-landscape medieval fantasy guild simulation centered on autonomous expeditions, persistent adventurers, relationships, recovery and generational legacy.

## Version
V1.5 — Character Stories

## Core loop
Recruit adventurers -> form a party -> plan a mission -> follow a connected expedition story -> review consequences -> return to a living guild -> grow characters, relationships and careers -> retire veterans -> continue through future generations.

## V1.5 — Character Stories
- Persistent personal story arcs driven by each adventurer's motivation
- Three milestone tiers per motivation
- Story milestones can become the main Camp event of an expedition
- Character detail now shows current arc, next milestone and completed story beats
- Important injuries can leave permanent scars with their own memories
- Story-derived titles appear alongside mechanical accomplishments
- Personal memories record milestones, recovery, visits and family moments

## Expeditions 2.0
- Travel and Exploration are now causally connected to the Encounter
- Route discoveries can modify success chance, injury risk and treasure outcomes
- Class composition, planning, traits and mission type influence the chain
- Camp and Return explicitly remember earlier expedition stages
- The final summary includes the expedition's narrative thread
- Expedition chronicle entries preserve the route/story that led to the result

## Story feedback
- The approved Story Theater composition remains unchanged
- Different mission types now add visual atmosphere to the central scene
- Party cards can visibly show prepared, injured and personal-milestone states
- Positive, danger and relationship feedback remain tied to real simulation outcomes
- Scene backgrounds remain procedural placeholders ready for future real artwork

## Living Guild
- Guild life now reacts to actual character state
- Companions may visit wounded adventurers
- Level-ups can trigger small celebrations
- Friends can train together between missions
- Rivalries can surface in guild arguments
- Couples can have quiet social moments
- Retired veterans can share stories with active adventurers
- Weekly and yearly time advances prioritize contextual guild moments

## V1.2 Story Theater retained
- Five-stage expedition ribbon
- Large central scene
- Persistent party cards
- Parchment-style event card
- Recent consequences
- Expedition log and final result cards
- Stable planning-header hotfix retained

## Core systems
- Six base classes and specializations
- Personality traits, origins and motivations
- Recruitment and persistent character histories
- Party chemistry and class synergies
- Mission planning: pace, priority and supplies
- Named injuries and recovery
- Guild infirmary, tavern, training yard and library
- Reputation-gated regions and contracts
- Friendship, rivalry, attraction, romance and marriage
- Retirement, families and descendants
- Multi-generation legacy
- Campaign diagnostics and browser save migration

## Tone
Grounded medieval fantasy with meaningful consequences but not grimdark. Danger, injury and death can occur, while the overall experience emphasizes adventure, community, recovery, friendship, family and legacy.

## Visual direction
The Story Theater remains the visual contract for expeditions. Future illustration work should replace procedural scene placeholders inside this composition rather than redesigning the screen.
